====================================================
        INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS
====================================================

1. What is Data?

Data is a collection of raw facts and figures that have not yet been processed or analyzed. It can be numbers, text, images, videos, or any other type of information collected from different sources.

Example:
Customer Name : Praveen
Product       : Laptop
Price         : ₹50,000
Date          : 14-07-2026

This is called data because it is just a record.

----------------------------------------------------

2. What is Information?

Information is processed and organized data that provides meaningful insights and helps people make better decisions.

Example:
- Laptop is the highest-selling product this month.
- Average daily sales are ₹25,000.
- Weekend sales are higher than weekday sales.

These are information because they are obtained after analyzing the data.

----------------------------------------------------

3. What is Data Analytics?

Data Analytics is the process of collecting, cleaning, transforming, analyzing, and interpreting data to discover useful insights and support business decisions.

The main goal of data analytics is to help businesses make informed decisions using data.

----------------------------------------------------

4. Who is a Data Analyst?

A Data Analyst is a professional who collects, cleans, analyzes, and visualizes data to solve business problems and provide valuable insights.

A Data Analyst converts raw data into meaningful information that helps organizations improve their performance.

----------------------------------------------------

5. What Does a Data Analyst Do?

A Data Analyst performs the following tasks:

• Collects data from different sources.
• Cleans and prepares the data.
• Analyzes the data to identify trends and patterns.
• Creates reports and dashboards.
• Presents insights to stakeholders.
• Helps businesses make informed decisions.

----------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------

7. Data Analytics Process

Business Problem
        ↓
Collect Data
        ↓
Clean Data
        ↓
Analyze Data
        ↓
Find Insights
        ↓
Create Reports & Dashboards
        ↓
Support Business Decisions

----------------------------------------------------

8. Example

Suppose I own a supermarket.

Every day I collect the following information:

• Customer Name
• Product Name
• Quantity
• Price
• Date of Purchase

These records are called DATA.

After analyzing one month's sales, I discover:

• Milk is the highest-selling product.
• Saturday has the highest sales.
• Hyderabad store generates the highest revenue.

These findings are called INFORMATION because they help me make business decisions.

----------------------------------------------------

 Key Points to Remember

• Data Analytics = Finding useful insights from data
• Data Analyst = Person who analyzes data and provides insights
• SQL = Works with databases
• Python = Data analysis and automation
• Power BI & Tableau = Data visualization
